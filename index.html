Copper & Crown: Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026. App version 0.1.34 and later.

Copper & Crown ("the game") is a single-player trading game for Android, made by Noodlebar Games. This page explains what the game does with information. The short version: it keeps your game on your phone, collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not use an internet connection.

What is stored on your phone

Your merchant's name and portrait, your progress and your settings are saved in a file on your phone. We cannot see this file. The merchant name is chosen by you and is only shown inside the game.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game can remind you when your offers fill, your workers finish or a buy limit resets. These reminders are created on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server. You can turn each kind off in Settings, Notifications, or in your phone's settings.

Phone backup

If your phone's backup is switched on, Android may back up the game's save file to your own Google account so it can be restored when you reinstall. That backup is handled by your phone and Google under Google's own privacy policy. The game and its developer never receive it.

Permissions

The game asks for permission to show notifications, vibrate for touch feedback, keep the phone awake briefly for reminders and restart reminders after the phone reboots. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, so there is nothing to collect from children either.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings, Account and choose "Delete all my data". You can also clear the game's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the game. Because nothing is sent to us, there is no copy of your data for us to delete.

Reporting a bug

The "Report a bug" button opens an email draft addressed to us containing the game version, your phone model, Android version and screen size. You can read and change the draft before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example when optional in-app purchases through Google Play are added, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
